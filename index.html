Built around how plants actually fail

Care that adapts to the room, not a spreadsheet

Most care apps give every pothos the same seven-day watering rule. Sprig factors in your specific window direction, the season, and how the plant has responded so far, and quietly adjusts the schedule as it learns.

A second opinion when something looks off

Overwatering and underwatering look almost identical at first — droopy, dull, unhappy. Sprig asks a few quick questions about what you're seeing and narrows down what's actually going on before you make it worse.
